# Problem Statement: Strategic AB Accounts

*Confidential (np)*

## The Opportunity

Strategic AB accounts — group accounts with >NZ$0.6M ARR TAM remaining — represent a **>$65M ARR opportunity through FY28**. These should be our highest-value, most consistent sales motion. Today, however, seller approach to these accounts varies widely by region, creating inconsistency and leaving value on the table.

## Regional Symptoms

### UK — Discount Cap Constraint
- A 20% discount cap compares unfavorably to QBO's 50% off from the first org.
- Xero partners need multiple orgs just to reach a 5% effective discount.
- Win/loss research flags price sensitivity and discount disparity as primary loss drivers.

### AU/NZ — Compressed Pricing Band
- Strategic partners already sit below rate card ($3.77 vs. $7.00 list).
- Annual ledger price rises apply uniformly, so the same percentage increase lands on our most strategic relationships just as it does on the long tail.
- This caps how aggressively we can move price for our most important accounts.

### US — Volume + ARPU Pressure
- Both deal volume and ARPU must grow materially to hit FY27 targets.
- Deal volume alone will not get us there — pricing and value capture must also improve.

## Key Pain Point

**Negotiation is the highest-friction phase and the largest source of value leakage across the Strategic Account process.**

This friction stems from a lack of consistent, robust deal infrastructure to support sellers, specifically around:

1. **Value sell** — TCO and incentives are not built into deal infrastructure, limiting clarity on positioning and differentiation.
2. **Platform sell** — There is no Financial Deal Model to support deep awareness of multi-product financial impact.
3. **Deal terms** — Ideal deal terms, including Financial KPI thresholds, are not clearly defined, and deal incentives don't yet support seller confidence.

## Where This Leads

The solution is **one shared deal infrastructure**: ideal deal terms, a simple deal model, TCO inputs, and streamlined governance — validated in the UK first, then expanded to AU and US.

With this infrastructure in place, Xero's strategic AB sellers can close deals faster and at higher value, with a digital CPQ system on the horizon to make this effortless at scale.
