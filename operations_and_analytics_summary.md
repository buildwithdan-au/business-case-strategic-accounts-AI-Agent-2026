# Operations & Analytics Summary: Strategic AB FY27 Planning

*Ownership & Delivery Detail*

## 1. Objective

Create a simple, consistent scorecard to track whether our Strategic AB program is being run as a **disciplined portfolio, not just a collection of large deals**.

## 2. Core Program Metrics

### a. Account Visibility — Coverage & Cadence
- Active account plan (NEAT) updated in last 90 days
  - Plan must include: objectives, value hypothesis, key plays, and next 90-day action
- Exec sponsor documented
  - Named Xero exec + partner exec; last interaction date and next planned touch
- QBR held in last 6 months
  - QBR notes or deck attached; clear decisions and follow-ups captured
- At least one open opportunity in current/next FY
  - New or expansion; linked to the account plan

**Target:** 90%+ of Strategic ABs in each region meeting all four conditions.

### b. Opportunity Visibility — Pipeline Quality
- Metric: % of Strategic AB opportunities that meet execution standards:
  - Next meeting scheduled (date, attendees, purpose)
  - Clear mutual next step documented in SFDC/Clari
  - Multi-threaded (≥2 contacts in buying group) where applicable

**Target:** 95%+ of Strategic AB opps with a next meeting and mutual next step.

### c. Mix & Penetration — Product Portfolio
- Definition: accounts with Core + at least one priority product (e.g. Syft, Payments, Payroll, other attach)
- Tracked at:
  - Account level: # of products per account
  - Region level: share of Strategic AB ARR from multi-product

**Targets (illustrative):**
- Year-on-year increase in products per Strategic AB
- ≥70% of Strategic AB ARR from multi-product accounts by FY27

## 3. How the Scorecard Is Used

**Inputs to cadences**
- **Global/Regional reviews:** scorecard is the first slide in the Strategic AB section
- **Manager reviews:** used to select accounts for deep dives (low-hygiene, high-potential)

**Actions driven**
- Regions below target must present a **simple remediation plan** (e.g. QBR blitz, plan refresh sprint)
- Individuals with repeated hygiene gaps get targeted coaching or support

## 4. Loop Back on Targets

**Dashboard/Report on Performance**
- **Closed Won ARR % to Target:** By region, how are we performing vs. expectations and ARR target?
- **Open Pipeline % to Target:** By region, what are we tracking towards vs. revenue target?
- **New Logo/Existing Business Mix:** By region, where are we driving ARR in Enterprise accounts?
